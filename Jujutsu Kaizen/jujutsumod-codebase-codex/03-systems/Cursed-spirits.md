# Cursed Spirits

Status: CURRENT (Cursed Saga landed on `feat/cursed-saga`; perception, grades, abilities and day/shelter spawning are in, balance is first-pass)

Three hostile cursed-spirit tiers — `lesser_cursed_spirit`, `cursed_spirit`, `greater_cursed_spirit` — whose **gameplay belongs to the tier** and whose **look belongs to a presentation variant** imported from the Sons of Sins asset pack (provenance in `docs/PROVENANCE.md`). One entity class, one variant enum, one profile: no per-variant gameplay.

## Shape

- `CursedSpiritEntity extends Monster` with the tier as a constructor argument (`CursedSpiritTier`), registered three times (`src/main/java/jujutsu/mod/registry/JujutsuEntities.java`); attributes via `FabricDefaultAttributeRegistry` in `CursedSpirits.registerServerHooks()`.
- Power no longer lives in the tier. Every body rolls an individual grade (`CursedSpiritGrade`: 5 weakest → 1 strongest, v1 spawns 5/4/3), and its HP/damage/speed finals (`CursedSpiritGradeStats`) are written into the attributes by `applyStats`. The tier row (`CursedSpiritTierStats`) now carries only the combat pattern — windup, cooldown, reach, knockback, lunge step, AoE radius and fraction, stagger, xp, spawn rows — and nudges the roll *inside* the grade band through `CursedSpiritTierArchetype`, never across it. A direct strike deals the rolled grade damage verbatim; the greater tier's AoE is that damage times the tier fraction.
- `CursedSpiritVariant` owns presentation *and* the in-tier spawn weight: PROWLER/FLOATING_CURSE/GULBER (lesser), KELVIN/BUTCHER/GUZZLER/BLUD (common), WALKING_BED/WISTIVER (greater). The variant is synced (ordinal) and persisted by **string id** under the NBT key `Variant`, validated against the tier on load — and it bends the ability roll, +2 weight to two signature abilities each (see Ability pools).
- Per-body state beyond tier and variant: the grade level (synced as `DATA_GRADE` — the only grade fact the client ever sees), the roll seed, the ability brain (pool + windows + cooldowns), and the latched berserk flag.
- Brain: `CursedSpiritAttackGoal` (APPROACH → WINDUP → STRIKE → RECOVER) reads live stats through `CursedSpiritAttackPolicy` (pure); target acquisition is perception-gated (selector predicate plus `setTarget` as the choke point, live targets that stop perceiving dropped on the next tick) and pauses while `CombatStagger.GLOBAL.isStaggered`. Regen retreat steers inside this goal, never as a second goal.
+
## Perception (issue #80)

A curse does not exist for a player who cannot perceive it — but only for that pair. Non-players always perceive: otherwise a curse would stop touching mobs, arrows and other curses the moment a lookup failed. Curse↔curse, curse↔mob and curse↔block simulation is never gated (acid still burns other spirits, slams still splash them); what is cut is exactly the non-perceiving-player ↔ curse-subject pair.

Who perceives is the vessel: `CharacterDefinition.cursePerception()` defaults to `PerceptionFlags.NONE`, and Megumi, Nobara and Todo override with `PERCEIVER`. Perceiving and interacting are two separate flags on purpose — a future Maki/Toji shape may hold one without the other. What counts as a curse is `CursePerception.isSubject`, the one subject test every gate uses: the `CursePerceptionSubject` marker or the `jujutsumod:curse_perception_subject` tag (the three spirit types plus the `cursed_acid_spit` glob).

The gates: targeting (selector predicate on `canInteract`, `setTarget` as the choke retaliation paths cannot bypass, a live target that stops perceiving dropped on the next tick), contact (`canCollideWith` plus `CursedSpiritPushMixin` on the shared `Entity.push` sink, so neither side displaces the other), damage both ways (`ALLOW_DAMAGE` refuses curse→non-perceiver and non-perceiver→curse) plus the melee-swing gate (`AttackEntityCallback` answers FAIL), network tracking (`CursedSpiritTrackingMixin` on `ChunkMap$TrackedEntity.updatePlayer` — no spawn packet, hence no client-side body to push against), voice (`makeSound` addresses one packet to each perceiver in range), and rendering (`CurseRenderGate`, which also gates the grade aura below). A non-perceiver walks through where a curse stands: unpushed, unhurt, hearing nothing, seeing nothing.

## Grades (issue #81)

Grades are the power axis over the tiers: 5/4/3 spawn weighted 60/30/10, grades 2–1 exist as model space only (every band lookup on them throws). One `rollSeed` per body feeds one stream consumed strictly as grade → variant → stats → abilities (`rollNewborn` performs the first three in one call so the order cannot drift; the ability stage replays from the seed and draws after). Each stat is the mean of 3 uniform draws inside its grade band — centre-concentrated, HP saying nothing about speed — plus the tier-archetype nudge (±half a band width, clamped inside), so grade order always wins and the old "lesser is fastest" identity survives only in morphology and pattern, not in numbers.

Bands (min / norm / max, `CursedSpiritGradeProfile`): HP 12–16–20 / 26–32–40 / 52–64–78; damage 3–4–5 / 6–7.5–9 / 11–13–16; speed 0.22–0.25 / 0.26–0.29 / 0.30–0.33 — disjoint across grades by construction. Persistence is `Grade` / `StatHp` / `StatDamage` / `StatSpeed` / `RollSeed` plus the pool and cooldowns (`Abilities`, `AbilityReadyAt_*`); active windows are transient and reset on reload, the berserk latch persists with its modifiers re-applied. A load with an unknown grade or out-of-band stats re-rolls fresh; wounds are preserved (fresh spawns top up, loads clamp to the new max).

The client never learns HP, damage or speed — only the grade level, which drives the aura (`CursedSpiritAura` / `CursedSpiritAuraRuntime`): 1 / 2 / 3 dust motes per tick, dull moss / teal / bright blue, growing size, and a soul-flame spark every 12 ticks on grade 3 alone. Aura emission passes through the render gate, so a non-perceiver sees neither body nor glow.

## Ability pools (issue #86)

Every spirit rolls three distinct abilities at spawn (weights = profile weight + variant signature, persisted with the body) and fights them through one `CursedSpiritAbilityBrain`: per-id windows and cooldowns, at most one new start per tick, two rules and no shared active id. Movement ownership (dash, slam, runner) keeps those three mutually exclusive and yields melee navigation while any runs; attack-clip occupancy (dash, slam, spit, runner, fear) blocks a new melee WINDUP while any plays. Regen, armor, berserk and live acid zones never hold the clip — a berserk spirit keeps swinging, harder. Armor and berserk are states and are never started; everything else is picked in combat by range, cooldown, weight and (for regen) need, or self-cast out of combat when hurt.

The eight, with grade-5 → grade-3 endpoints (`CursedSpiritAbilityProfile.of`, the only tuning surface — field meaning is per-id in `CursedSpiritAbilityParams`): dash, a frozen-direction impulse at 0.9 → 1.1 blocks/tick with collision damage 4 → 9 (range 4–16, never re-aimed); ground slam, a jump plus a body-centred shockwave 5 → 12 over radius 3 → 4 that also catches other spirits but never a non-perceiving player; acid spit, a straight-flying glob (direct hit 3 → 6) that lands a static zone (radius 2 → 3, lifetime 100 → 140 ticks, pulse every 10, 2 → 4 per pulse, 3 zones per owner, creator immune — `AcidZoneRuntime`, `cursed_acid` damage, green-cube renderer over `textures/entity/cursed_acid_spit.png`); grab-runner, a player-only snatch carried ~4 s for displacement, never damage, denying attacks, block breaking and block placement while routing around lava, drops and walls; fear, `DARKNESS` + `NAUSEA` + the invisible `CURSED_FEAR` marker for 100 → 180 ticks (range 8 → 12) while the client inverts keys (physical W↔S), mouse buttons (0↔1), wheel and look — text entry untouched; regen, a heal-over-time window (120 → 160 ticks, 1 → 2 per 20-tick pulse, considered at ≤60% HP) that walks the body away from its target; armor, flat absorption 2 → 4 applied before `super.hurtServer`, a fully blocked hit staying silent; berserk, latching permanently once damage drops the body to ≤35% HP for +10 → 15% speed and +30 → 50% damage. Nine `curse/*` VFX cues (one per ability plus the zone pulse) carry the telegraphs; delivery radius 48.

## Combat-core ties (shared systems, no new ones)

- Stagger: `jujutsu.mod.combat.StaggerResistant` — `CharacterCombatModifiers.adjustedStaggerTicks` asks the body; the tier multiplier floors at 1 tick.
- Boogie Woogie: greater spirits carry `jujutsumod:boogie_woogie_immune` and `TodoBoogieWoogieRuntime.isEligibleTarget` refuses them; lesser/common swap like any monster.
- Nails/resonance: ordinary nail impacts mark and (threshold 2) mint a remnant; a cursed spirit's type is in `jujutsumod:resonance_remnant_curse`, so the minted visual is CURSE (`RemnantVisualType.classify`).
- Megumi: sic resolves cursed spirits as ordinary eligible targets.

## Rendering (deliberate deviation from the GeckoLib house style)

The pack ships vanilla-API Java models and `AnimationDefinition` classes, so the client stack is a **near-verbatim vanilla port** (`src/client/java/jujutsu/mod/client/render/cursedspirit/**`): 9 `EntityModel<CursedSpiritRenderState>` models with one bake per rig, 9 animation classes, one `CursedSpiritRenderer` (three instances). The models no longer pick their own clip: `CursedSpiritClips.apply` arbitrates once per frame (`scream > attack > idle + walk`, tolerant of missing scream/walk), so a swing is never overwritten by the idle loop mid-animation and a repeated hit extends the scream instead of restarting the hurt window (issue #77) that dispatches the per-variant rig the way `TropicalFishRenderer` does (`this.model = rigs.get(state.variant); super.render(...)`), and `CursedSpiritClient.register()` for layers + renderers. State sync uses the vanilla entity-event channel with ids in a private high range: `ATTACK_START=100`, `SCREAM_START=101`, `ATTACK_END=102`, `SCREAM_END=103` — the range matters, because `ClientPacketListener.handleEntityEvent` casts ids 21 (Guardian), 35 (totem) and **63 (Sniffer) unconditionally, and 63 used to be `ATTACK_END`: any client near a spirit that finished an attack was disconnected with `ClassCastException` (found by the live pass, fixed 2026-09-12, pinned by `CursedSpiritAnimationStateTest`) (stop-then-start on starts, `stop()` on ends, `SCREAM_DURATION_TICKS=12`); FLOATING_CURSE glides (the pack authored no walk clip) and `WISTIVER.GRAVE` is not shipped (no driver state).
+
Perception also owns the client picture: `CurseRenderGate` draws subjects for perceivers only (fail-open without a viewer), the tracking filter means a non-perceiver never even receives the spawn packet, and `makeSound` re-addresses the voice per perceiver. New render-side bodies: the per-grade aura above, and the acid glob — the stone renderer's cube skeleton with a sickly green tint and a faster spin, never another grey rock.

## Spawning

- Natural pipeline only: `BiomeModifications.addSpawn(foundInOverworld(), MONSTER, type, weight, min, max)` from `CursedSpiritSpawnIntegration` — **no** `SpawnPlacements` (its `register` is private in 1.21.8 and there is no Fabric wrapper) and no access widener.
- The gate lives in `CursedSpiritEntity.checkSpawnRules`: explicit `Difficulty.PEACEFUL → false`, then `super` (`PathfinderMob`'s walk-value light gate — refuses at local brightness ≳12, dim 8–12 passes; deliberately not re-implementing vanilla's rolled darkness), then the local crowd cap `MAX_SPIRITS_NEARBY = 10` within `CROWD_RADIUS = 48` **for `EntitySpawnReason.NATURAL` only** — a spawner, spawn egg or command places its spirit regardless of the crowd, vanilla-consistent because population pressure governs natural spawning (and it keeps the cap conjunct out of a shared-level GameTest oracle).
- No daylight burning (plain `Monster`; they spawn dark and persist); peaceful spawns nothing.
+
- Daylight is no absolute ban (#82). `CursedSpiritSpawnSchedule` allows night unconditionally and day on a 0.6 roll (`DAY_SPAWN_CHANCE`, rollback switch 0.0); time never touches the tier/grade roll, only frequency. `checkSpawnRules` reads difficulty through the pure `difficultyAllows` predicate (PEACEFUL refuses — testable without flipping the shared global difficulty), skips the crowd cap off the natural path as before, and after a refused vanilla light half still allows the spawn on a passed day roll — day only, a night refusal stays refused.
- Daytime shelter (#82): a 0..1 score, not a sky boolean — open sky 0, a ceiling within 3 air blocks 1.0, roofed-but-roomy grading 0.4–0.6 by remaining skylight. `CursedSpiritShelterGoal` (priority 5, `MOVE + JUMP`: below melee 2 so a target always wins, above stroll 7) walks a day-and-out-of-combat body scoring below 0.5 to the best nearby cell and settles at 0.9. Combat and night silence it completely — no running to a tree mid-fight. Scans are bounded (8-block ring, 48 candidates, 40-tick period) and walk at speed 1.0.

## Verification boundary

Automated: profile/variant/policy/registry/tag JUnit, resource + sound-symmetry contracts, rig/animation contracts (clip keys, bone sets, looping flags, clip counts, exact lengths), and GameTests for AI/strike/AoE/knockback/swap/stagger/cap/spawn-light plus the cross-character integration scenarios (nail mark → Hairpin, remnant mint, Black Flash, sic).
+
Cursed Saga extends the automated side: perception pair-gates (target, damage, melee, push, tracking), grade bands/rolls/NBT round-trip and the re-roll fallback, the ability matrix (classification, pool trio, start rules, per-effect scenarios), schedule/shelter math, and GameTests for the same plus day-spawn and shelter-seeking/silence oracles.

Manual only (cannot be automated headless): sound firing/coherence per tier, animation timing feel, variant distinctness and silhouette read at distance, world spawn feel + soak comfort (census ≤ cap, TPS ≥ 18, zero `jujutsumod` ERROR), hitbox visual fit, and the per-tier menace of a greater spirit. Run them with the MCP dev lane; the protocol and the tool quirks live in `.superpowers/rule-of-four/cursed-spirits/live-protocol.md`.

## Tuning pointers

Balance: `CursedSpiritProfile` (tier pattern rows + crowd cap) and `CursedSpiritGradeProfile` (bands, 60/30/10, nudge spread) plus `CursedSpiritTierArchetype` ranks. Look/scale/weights/sounds: `CursedSpiritVariant`. Attack feel: policy/goal read both, so a number change is global. Ability feel: `CursedSpiritAbilityProfile.of` per-grade rows, decide ranges and trigger fractions. Spawn pressure: the three `addSpawn` rows + `MAX_SPIRITS_NEARBY` + `DAY_SPAWN_CHANCE`; shelter eagerness: `WANT_BELOW` / `SETTLED_AT`. Aura look: `CursedSpiritAuraRuntime` (pending the live-review verdict). All balance is first-pass — treat the tables as the tuning surface, not as a contract.
